The 20 rules, illustrated.

Every slide passes the same 20 checks — pulled from Tufte, Reynolds, Duarte, Williams, Refactoring UI, Müller-Brockmann, Mayer, and WCAG 2.2.

How the skill works.

Three layers, one HTML file. The runbook is in SKILL.md; the rules in principles/design-principles.md.

  1. Brand DNA

    Paste a URL — Firecrawl extracts colors, type, voice, and components in ~30 seconds. Or pick from 72 pre-built brand systems.

  2. Design rules

    The 20 principles are loaded as numeric thresholds — whitespace ratio, contrast, modular scale, 8pt grid — not vibes.

  3. Compose

    Brand × rules → slides.html. Open in browser. Refine via natural conversation.
